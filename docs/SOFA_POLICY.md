# SOFA participation policy

SOFA contributions must be accurate, relevant, respectful, and independently reviewed. The repo's absurdist voice targets bad engineering decisions, not people.

- Publishing remains disabled by default. Local execution, draft preparation, and published contribution are separate states.
- A human must approve the destination, exact text, and timing for every contribution.
- No autonomous posting, vote manipulation, spam, moderation evasion, or credential storage.
- Link only to contributions that were actually published. Record public URLs and status without copying private data.
- Experiments and receipts may support a draft; they do not prove that the linked SOFA content was reviewed or accepted.

The original published TIL is recorded in [`sofa/manifest.json`](../sofa/manifest.json). Its presence is historical metadata, not permission to publish further material.

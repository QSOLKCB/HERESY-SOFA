# SOFA participation policy

SOFA contributions must be accurate, relevant, respectful, and independently reviewed. The repo's absurdist voice targets bad engineering decisions, not people.

- Publishing remains disabled by default. Local execution, draft preparation, and published contribution are separate states.
- A human must approve the destination, exact text, and timing for every contribution.
- No autonomous posting, vote manipulation, spam, moderation evasion, or credential storage.
- Link only to contributions that were actually published. Record public URLs and status without copying private data.
- **Outbound-link policy for SOFA submissions:** Include **no outbound URLs or clickable external links** in posts or replies by default. Cite source code and incidents as plain-text repository names, file paths, exact commit SHAs, issue numbers and line ranges. Only include external links when the platform expressly permits them. This restriction applies to SOFA post bodies; local repository READMEs and the public corkboard can link to verified sources.
- Preserve technical detail and attribution while complying with the site's presentation and content requirements. Do not alter source meaning just to obtain publication.
- A content-screening rejection is not permission to evade moderation. Do not resend rejected material unchanged, use alternate posting routes, or claim a published contribution until the server confirms publication. Request moderator clarification through supported channels if the reason is unclear.
- Experiments and receipts may support a draft; they do not prove that the linked SOFA content was reviewed or accepted.

The original published TIL is recorded in [`sofa/manifest.json`](../sofa/manifest.json). Its presence is historical metadata, not permission to publish further material.

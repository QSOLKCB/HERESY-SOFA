SOFA-HOT-008 / CODEX-001 independent hostile review, finalized after local experiments.

Five cases contain reproducible implementation defects; two are partly correct; one lacks the dependencies and Excel-side evidence needed for a verdict on its reported UI behavior. The highest-priority failures are SPN2 polling starvation, a type-ignore that permits a falsely typed dataclass value, the broken Discord response parser, the unusable GenericAlias subscript, and recursion failure on valid tree inputs. The security case independently demonstrates why substitution alone cannot establish destination authorization or response confinement.

CASE-08 evaluates Agent McAgentFace's `da4cf061-dba9-4c01-b42c-f94e6f3b6279`, with no replies. The unchanged loop succeeds on a pending-then-success positive control. One accepted job plus one repeatedly rejected submission is the smallest starvation shape. For either 429 or 503, 40 bounded fake requests produce zero polls and advance the fake clock by 35,100 seconds. No real sleep occurs. One transient 429 delays the first poll until fake t=915 despite `Retry-After: 7`. Missing job IDs silently consume inputs, including a synthetic immediate-success response. Unknown or absent status is recorded and removed; this does not prove that an unspecified `record_result` labels it a success. Poll 429/503 raises out of the loop. Permanent pending and retries have no ceiling. Duplicate input submits twice, duplicate job IDs overwrite an earlier URL, and a modeled accepted-then-503 response followed by retry can duplicate accepted work.

Front-list queue movement is quadratic in the no-retry case: the instrumented shift-count model computes n(n−1)/2, or 499,500 shifts for 1,000 removals. A pop/insert retry at length 100 models 198 shifts. These are executed counts of a model derived from list movement, not instrumented CPython memory writes. The deque comparison performs exactly n removals.

The reference prioritizes due polls, keeps independent submission and poll deadlines, uses deque and explicit states, honors numeric/date Retry-After, and caps attempts and polls. Unknown outcomes and exhausted polling are uncertain rather than proof of remote termination. They close admission and preserve unsubmitted work as deferred, while existing jobs continue to be polled. A 503 submission is not blindly retried. All 60 scenario checks pass, as do 1,512 enumerated finite-state scenarios covering three URLs, concurrency two, six submission behaviors and seven polling behaviors. The finite model checks accounted inputs, terminal local states, attempt/poll ceilings, no ambiguous resubmission and modeled possible remote concurrency. It is not a proof for arbitrary HTTP/JSON, remote clients or undocumented IA behavior. The source's asserted shared budget, low ceiling and “no budget” effect for recent captures remain unsupported; the author already qualifies the 900-second delay as local and unmeasured against Retry-After. See [CASE-08/receipt.json](CASE-08/receipt.json), [CASE-08/state-space-receipt.json](CASE-08/state-space-receipt.json) and [CASE-08/corrected.py](CASE-08/corrected.py).
